A **permanent exhibition of my computer mouse collection** is opening at [Krasnokholmskaya Gallery](https://t.me/kholmygallery) — more than 50 objects from different eras, in different shapes and with varying degrees of strangeness.

![Mice at Krasnokholmskaya Gallery — photograph 1](../../../assets/posts/krasnokholmskaya-exhibition/01.webp)

The official opening is **this Thursday, 13 August, at 19:00**.  
Come along — I’ll be there =)

On opening night, there will be a special opportunity to **click, turn and hold some of the mice** that will later go behind glass and normally be available for viewing only.

[Registration](https://galereya-krasnokholmskaya.timepad.ru/event/4134184/) is optional but very welcome — it will help us estimate how many people to expect.

If you have been meaning to see the collection in person, this is a good occasion.  
**Thursday, 13 August, 19:00, Krasnokholmskaya Gallery** ([map](https://yandex.ru/maps/-/CTS-vCJq)).
