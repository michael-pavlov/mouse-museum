A processor module from the 1975 [Amdahl 470V/6](https://www.fujitsu.com/global/about/corporate/history/products/computer/mainframe/facom470v6.html) mainframe. [Gene Amdahl](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%90%D0%BC%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%BB,_%D0%94%D0%B6%D0%B8%D0%BD) first designed System/360, then left IBM and launched his own line of compatible machines that were cheaper and faster.
