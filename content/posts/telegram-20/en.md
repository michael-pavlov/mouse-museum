The most hardcore mouse mechanically.

![Graphic Mouse — photo 1](../../../assets/posts/telegram-20/01.webp)

Its sensors are potentiometers with fixed minimum and maximum positions. An adjustment on the underside lets you align the screen boundaries with the mouse's virtual working area.

![Graphic Mouse — photo 2](../../../assets/posts/telegram-20/02.webp)

A belt drive! And the belts haven't dried out over all these years, as they usually do in audio equipment. Have a look at that pressure spring, too.

There are no brains inside. Electronically, this is more of an analogue joystick than a conventional mouse.

![Graphic Mouse — photo 3](../../../assets/posts/telegram-20/03.webp)

It all looks like a prototype, but quite a few were produced under different names and with different button colours. This version is for the Apple II; Commodore versions existed as well.

![Graphic Mouse — photo 4](../../../assets/posts/telegram-20/04.webp)
