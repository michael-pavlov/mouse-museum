The [Yandex Museum Festival](https://clck.ru/3PA4ZB) is approaching, and we continue introducing its participants.

![The evolution of the computer mouse — photo 3](../../../assets/posts/telegram-208/03.webp)

Today, with collector [Michael Pavlov](https://t.me/old_mice_and_hw), we use objects from his collection to trace the evolution of computer mice, from Xerox's first commercial optical devices to Soviet Martian and Kolobok mice. We'll discover why Microsoft made mice with green eyes, why Penny & Giles added fabric inserts, and how Swiss engineers won over the world.
