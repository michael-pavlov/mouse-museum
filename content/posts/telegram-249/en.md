5 September: mice at FestTech!

![Mice at FestTech — photo 1](../../../assets/posts/telegram-249/01.webp)

This Saturday, the mice and I will be at [FestTech](https://festtech.ru/), the science and technology festival at the ATOM Museum at VDNKh.

I'll bring a small travelling exhibition: some mice will live in a display case, while others, as usual, can be picked up, clicked, turned and examined from every angle.

An important point: this will be a different set of mice from those currently in the permanent exhibition at Krasnokholmskaya Gallery. Even if you've been there already, there will be something new to see here :)

I'll be there too, of course. Come and say hello, ask questions and hear strange stories about why people have spent the last half-century inventing new ways to move a cursor around a screen.

Admission is free. [Register here](https://festtechfestival.timepad.ru/event/4094682/).

Come and try the mice 🐁
