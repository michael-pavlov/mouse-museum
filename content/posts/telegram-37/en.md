The exhibition of computer mice and trackballs continues!

![An evening at the museum: Mousetrap — photo 1](../../../assets/posts/telegram-37/01.webp)

Alongside the Mousetrap exhibition, we are holding a themed Evening at the Museum on 24 June. Our colleague, mouse and trackball collector Michael Pavlov, will explain why these particular devices fascinate him and how he finds new additions to his collection. We start at 19:00: [register and come along](https://forms.yandex.ru/surveys/13473772.b571e25d117459ff8514c524a4d59d0a94a3fb24/?utm_source=tg&utm_medium=yandexmuseum&utm_campaign=20june&utm_content=post)!

The exhibition itself runs until 12 July. Michael has collected more than 150 mice and trackballs over ten years. You can see most of them at the Yandex Museum near Paveletskaya, and explore the whole collection on the [exhibition website](https://yandex.ru/museum/mouses?utm_source=tg&utm_medium=yandexmuseum&utm_campaign=20june&utm_content=post).

Moscow, Aurora Business Centre, 82 Sadovnicheskaya Street, Building 2.
