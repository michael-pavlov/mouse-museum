A fairly rare mouse from the Minsk Computer Plant. I couldn't find any mention online of pointing-device variants for the 1849.

![ES 1849 — photo 1](../../../assets/posts/telegram-38/01.webp)

Its circuit design is similar to the [ES 1841](https://collectionerus.ru/collections/old-mice/8/).

![ES 1849 — photo 2](../../../assets/posts/telegram-38/02.webp)

The case is a step forward, certainly, but it feels very fragile, inside and out. Everything rattles; nothing is properly secured.

![ES 1849 — photo 3](../../../assets/posts/telegram-38/03.webp)
