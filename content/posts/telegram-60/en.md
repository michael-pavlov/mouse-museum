Modern trackballs also use optical sensors. But while mouse sensors take pictures of the surface beneath them, trackballs don't have that kind of surface.

![The Megatron optical trackball — photo 1](../../../assets/posts/telegram-60/01.webp)

The ball itself serves as the surface, so early models had patterned balls. This is especially obvious on the red balls of older Logitech models.

I have an embedded [Megatron trackball](https://collectionerus.ru/collections/old-mice/160/) whose ball pattern might make an uninitiated observer think it was assembled from rubbish. The ball is also covered in transparent plastic.
