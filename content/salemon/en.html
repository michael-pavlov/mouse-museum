A few
questions.

Is this only for mouse collectors?+

No. You can search for vintage tech, books, coins and other things. The marketplace must be supported by the bot, and your search link should include your keywords and filters.

Which marketplaces are supported?+

Avito, eBay, Yahoo! Auctions, Buyee, OLX, Allegro and more. Check whether a particular marketplace is available when you add a link in the bot.

How do I make my search precise?+

Configure your search on the marketplace: keywords, category, price, location and any available filters. Then copy the results URL. Matching accuracy depends on the marketplace’s search and the seller’s description.

How is this different from saved searches?+

SaleMonitor checks your search every 60, 30 or 10 seconds and sends new listings straight to Telegram. You don’t need to keep visiting the marketplace and refreshing the results.

Am I guaranteed to buy first?+

A fast alert helps you spot a listing sooner. The purchase still depends on the seller, other buyers and your agreement. The service does not reserve or buy items for you.

What happens after the three free days?+

To keep using the service, choose a plan in the bot: $25 per month for checks every 60 seconds, $50 for every 30 seconds, or $125 for every 10 seconds.
